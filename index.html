Connecting a Growing Phoenix Through Public Transportation

Phoenix is one of the largest cities in the United States, with more than 1.6 million residents and approximately 4,850 miles of Public streets. Because the city covers a large Transportation network, public tansit plays an important role in helping residents travel to work, school, services, and other destinations. This data story looks public transportation in Phoenix and examines how transit helps people connect across a large and growing city. Using information from the city of phoenix and related public transportation sources, The story will compare transport data and show how phoenix's transit system supports moblilty throughout the city.
